# 01 · 杂志封面贴纸网点海报（日系平涂赛璐璐 × 杂志排版 × 贴纸拼贴）

> **风格定位**：日系平涂赛璐璐打底 + 时尚杂志封面式编辑排版 + 模切贴纸白边层 + 日式漫画网点阴影的**角色 PV / 动态壁纸海报**——「各样元素堆砌的精致平涂动漫 PV 感」。平面设计感与排版文字是主体，**不是自然场景、也不是普通插画**。
> **适用**：anima（tag + 自然语言交并，纯英文）｜**提交**：`workflows/anima/anima_normal_template.py --prompt "…" --w … --h … --name …`（分辨率只改 [19]，详见 `workflows/anima/guide.md`）。
> 本文件属 `prompt-library/anima/` 风格库（风格①）。

## 一、八个旋钮（命名体系——用来点单，不必描述画面）

下次只需报旋钮名（「杂志封面排版 + 贴纸白边 + 网点赛璐璐，前景加 Q 版贴纸小人，左红竖块跨块换色标题」），改动只点一个旋钮即只改那一段，**不整篇重写**。

| # | 旋钮 | 中文说法 | 英文词眼 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 底子画风 | 日系平涂赛璐璐（商业向） | `clean cel shading`, `flat colour fills`, `uniform lineart`, `glossy digital illustration finish` |
| 2 | 版式 | 时尚杂志封面排版 | `fashion magazine cover layout`, `modular editorial grid`, headline spanning the top edge |
| 3 | 文字手法 | **跨色块换色标题**（同一行字跨越色块边界：块内反白、块外转黑） | `letters crossing a colour bar, reversed out in white inside the block, solid black outside, switching exactly at the edge` |
| 4 | 装饰 | **模切贴纸白边** + 贴纸轰炸 | `die-cut sticker with a thick white paper border`, `sticker-bomb collage` |
| 5 | Q 版层 | Q 版贴纸小人（前景层） | `miniature chibi sticker doubles` |
| 6 | 网点 | 日式漫画网点阴影（疏密可调） | `manga screentone halftone dots`／疏 `widely spaced dots`／密 `dense dot clusters` |
| 7 | 配色 | 三色海报硬边配色 | `three-colour poster palette, flat colour with hard edges`, `no gradients` |
| 8 | 壁纸预览结构 | 四角贴纸装饰 + 底部图标栏（桌面壁纸预览那一类构图） | `corner decorations pinned around the edges`, `a dark translucent dock bar along the bottom edge with rounded app icons` |

> 第 3 号是本风格**最有辨识度、也最容易丢**的手法；第 4 号「白边」与第 6 号「网点」是两回事，别混着说。

## 二、分层防混淆写法（P0——三层混一起就废）

堆砌型提示词里，模型最容易把前景 Q 版、主体、背景图案揉成一团。**三层各写一段、段首带层名，并在词里写明各自的尺寸地位**：

1. **主体层**——先声明唯一性与占比，再写角色串与动作：
   `MAIN SUBJECT — the only full-size figure, the largest element in the frame:` + `(1girl:1.3)` + 角色串 + 动作。
   第一句就锁死"唯一全尺寸人物"，模型才不会把 Q 版画成第二个人。
2. **Q 版层**——尺寸 + 否定句双保险：
   `FOREGROUND — tiny chibi sticker layer, close to the camera:` + `only a small fraction of the main figure's height` + `clearly stickers rather than a second full-size character` + 厚白边 + 投影。
3. **背景层**——明确"是平面图案、不是场景、对比度低于主体"：
   `BACKGROUND — flat magazine graphic printed behind her, lower in contrast than the figure, not a scene:`。
   不写 `not a scene`，模型会去画一间真房间。
4. 层与层之间**空行分段**——分段本身就是给模型的注意力分区；人物串后绝不补外貌词（样貌由角色串固定）。

## 三、文字 / 网点 / 配色策略

- **文字**：每处只放 1–2 个**全大写短词**（`PLANA`、`BLUE`、`ARCHIVE`、`07`），整句必崩；字要"可读"就用引号包住该词（`"PLANA"`）。崩字就先砍到 3 处：大标题 + 侧栏 + 大数字。
- **跨块换色句固定写法**（案例 2 的核心，**不可改写**）：
  `one line of very large bold capital letters crossing that bar from left to right so that the letters inside the bar are reversed out in white while the same letters outside the bar are printed solid black, the colour switching exactly at the edge of the bar`
  想换色序就把 `white` 与 `black` 对调。
- **网点**：默认**稀疏**（`widely spaced dots rather than dense clusters, light dot screens laid only over the deepest shadows`）；要密就换 `dense dot clusters packed into the deepest shadows`。
- **配色**：三色硬边（红 / 米白 / 深黑，冷蓝灰作唯一副色）+ `no gradients`；换主色只改 COLOUR 段三个颜色名。
- 要凑量时优先加"层次与物证"（网格线、幽灵字、印章、条形码标签），不要堆形容词。

### 案例 1 · 极近特写壁纸预览（V 手势大头 × 四角 Q 版贴纸 × 底部图标栏）

**【类型】** 桌面壁纸预览式角色海报 ｜ **【比例】** 横宽 2.197:1（[19] 建议 `1616×736`，约 1.19M 像素）｜ **【文字策略】** 完全禁文字（原文排除段已含）｜ **【核心手法】** 脸 + 双手占满约 4/5 画面、几乎零留白；四角钉贴纸式小元素（Q 版头像 / 圆贴纸 / 亚克力立牌 / 道具）；底部半透明图标栏收口 ｜ **【复用要点】** 换个角色只改角色串；四角元素与底部栏是整个构图的结构，别单独删。

```
STYLE — clean cel-shaded anime:
modern Japanese anime cel-shading, crisp digital lineart of perfectly uniform thickness, smooth vector-clean outlines, slightly heavier line weight around the face to define the features, flat colour fills with only a small amount of soft airbrushed shading on the cheeks and under the chin, soft blurred shadow edges, no screentone, no halftone dots, no grain texture, high-saturation bright colours, glossy clean digital illustration finish, fresh and airy

COLOUR:
a dominant pale yellow-green and spring-green palette, soft peach-pink skin tones, golden accents on the accessories and in the highlights, cool teal shadows, a few deep plum-black edges around the eyes, high brightness with low contrast, the whole image lit like lemonade light

SUBJECT — the character, written as action only:
Plana from Blue Archive, plana_(blue_archive), solo, 1girl, an extreme close-up in which only her head, shoulders and both hands are inside the frame, both hands raised beside her cheeks making V-signs with the fingertips just below her eyes, mouth slightly open, leaning in toward the viewer, gazing straight into the camera lens, her hair swaying a little as she leans forward

CAMERA / COMPOSITION:
tight headshot framing, her face and hands filling roughly four fifths of the entire frame, almost no negative space anywhere, centred composition with her face directly in the middle of the image, eye-level camera, flat shallow depth, wide horizontal wallpaper format

BACKGROUND:

CORNER DECORATIONS — pinned around the edges like wallpaper stickers:
a half-cropped chibi head illustration tucked into the top left corner; a round hanging sticker at the top right containing two stacked chibi faces; a clear acrylic standee at the bottom left showing a full-body standing illustration of the same girl, a slice of orange beside it and a white polka-dot backing behind it; a tiny white chibi figure at the bottom right with a silver fork and a lemon dessert on a plate next to it

a dark translucent dock bar running along the very bottom edge of the frame, filled with rounded rectangular app icons in blue, green and red, one thin light indicator line centred underneath it

### 案例 2 · 杂志封面贴纸网点海报（跳跃欢呼 × Q 版微缩贴纸 × 左红竖块跨块换色标题）

**【类型】** 杂志封面式角色 PV 海报 / 竖版动态壁纸 ｜ **【比例】** 竖 9:16（[19] 建议 `832×1472`，约 1.22M 像素；横版桌面用 `1440×810`，红竖块与文字位置不必改）｜ **【文字策略】** 短词大写（`07`、跨块标题词），原文未给排除段，注入文字时可自加 `exclude: text, letters…` 之外只保留点名要写的词 ｜ **【核心手法】** 三层分离（主体唯一性声明 × Q 版微缩贴纸 × 平面杂志背景）；左侧红竖块 + 标题跨块换色；稀疏网点只压在深阴影 ｜ **【复用要点】** 换标题词只改引号内、换色只改 COLOUR 段、换网点疏密只改 SCREENTONE 段。

```
STYLE:
modern Japanese anime illustration, clean cel shading, flat colour fills with soft airbrushed shading, crisp lineart of uniform thickness, glossy digital illustration finish, high saturation bright colours, vivid and clean

## 五、复用要点（换要素时只动一处）

| 想换的东西 | 只改哪里 |
|---|---|
| 角色 | 所有 `Plana from Blue Archive, plana_(blue_archive)` → 目标角色串（外貌仍不另补） |
| 标题词 | 引号内的短词（`"PLANA"` / `"07"`） |
| 主色 | `COLOUR:` 段的三个颜色名 |
| 网点疏密 | `SCREENTONE:` 段的疏/密词眼 |
| 比例 | [19] 分辨率（竖 832×1472 / 横 1440×810 / 超宽 1616×736），版式段落一般不必改 |
| Q 版动作 / 数量 | `FOREGROUND` 段的 pose 与数量（保持"微缩 + 白边 + 非真人"三件套） |
| 跨块换色 | 把 `white` 与 `black` 对调；**整句结构不动** |
| 主体动作 | `SUBJECT:` 段的动作链；人物串与角色串保持原样 |
